# 2026 Skåne bike ban

Documentation of Region Skåne's decision to ban bikes from boarding Pågatåg and Öresundståg at peak times, from 1 November 2026: where it came from, what it was based on, and what the public record shows.

The goal is to publish everything as a public website on GitHub Pages. Files are plain Markdown and CSV so they can be published and processed as they are.

## Folder structure

| Folder / file | What goes in it |
| --- | --- |
| `inputs/press/` | Press and media coverage, now and historical. `index.md` (readable) and `press.csv` (for scripts). Saved article copies. |
| `inputs/notes/` | Private research notes from chats, calls and tips, dated (`notes.md`) |
| `inputs/contacts/` | `contacts.csv`: names, roles, emails, phones and sources for everyone we may contact |
| `inputs/legal/` | Laws, EU regulation, agreements and travel conditions; `index.md` |
| `inputs/political_decisions/` | Minutes, decision papers, reports from regions and municipalities, government communication; `index.md`, `transcripts/` |
| `outputs/requests/` | Public-records requests ready to send (one folder per recipient, with `.eml` drafts and a `svar/` folder for replies) and `log.csv` |
| `analysis/` | Legal and political assessment |
| `timeline.md` | Dated chronology |

File naming for saved originals: `YYYY-MM-DD_source_short-title.ext`. Originals are never edited.

## Key facts so far

- **Decision:** kollektivtrafiknämnden, 1 October 2026, agenda item 6, case **2022-POL000109**.
- **Ban:** non-holiday weekdays, 06:00–08:00 and 15:30–17:30 (narrowed at the meeting from 06–09 and 15–18), boarding at all Skåne stations.
- **Same case number** was opened on 7 April 2022 to *increase* bike access on Pågatåg. The only report-back was a three-slide deck (October 2022) with no data, which said bikes did not compete with places for disabled passengers. It led to a new case, 2022-POL000362, to review the rules; no outcome found.
- **The only document in the 2026 case file** is the chair's proposal dated 24 September 2026. No data, no legal review, no consultation with unions or partner regions before the decision.
- **Staff union figures (Seko klubb Pågatåg):** 29 bike incidents in 2026, 6 at rush hour.
- **Minutes for item 6 were to be approved immediately**, so the three-week appeal window may open within days.
