# Case assessment

As of 2026-10-01. Not legal advice. Full version with more detail: [Claude doc](https://claude.ai/code/artifact/dfe4afe5-8bdb-4f4c-8597-c32ff0aa357e).

## Bottom line

A court is unlikely to stop the ban before 1 November, but there is a real case worth filing, and a realistic chance of changing it politically.

| Outcome | Chance | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Court suspends ban before 1 Nov | Low | Suspension rarely granted; a bike rule can be reversed overnight |
| Court annuls the decision later | Low to moderate | Depends on what the minutes and case file show |
| EU passenger-rights route stops it | Very low | Art. 6(3) of 2021/782 allows peak-hour capacity restrictions |
| Ban shortened, softened or postponed | Moderate | Hours already cut at the meeting; partner regions, Denmark, opposition have leverage |

## What the decision papers show

- **One document only** in the case: the chair's proposal of 2026-09-24. No data on crowding or incidents.
- **No legal review.** No mention of EU Regulation 2021/782 or the Öresundståg cooperation agreement.
- **Partner regions and Öresundståg AB informed only after** the decision (compare clause 2.5.2).
- **Ban limited to Skåne stations**; not outside Skåne or in Denmark. Weakens the territorial argument.
- **Unions not consulted**; operator "informed". Seko klubb Pågatåg reports 29 incidents in 2026, only 6 at rush hour.
- **Case 2022-POL000109 was opened to increase bike access**; the September 2022 report is not referred to.
- **The rush-hour ban was already on the table in 2023 as a fallback, and was set aside in 2024.** In February 2023 (§ 15, 2022-POL000362) the committee tightened the rules and listed a rush-hour ban as an escalation step if they failed. The February 2024 report-back (§ 21, 8 March 2024) found complaints down 25 %, said the problems could be handled within the existing rules, and reserved route- and time-specific bans for problems reaching a "justifiable" level. The 2026 proposal does not refer to this assessment, gives no data showing the threshold was reached, and goes further (all Skåne stations) than the targeted options named in 2024. This strengthens the "not lawfully made" (poor preparation) ground.

## Legal grounds (KL 13:8), strongest first

| Ground | Argument | Strength |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 3. No power to decide | Öresundståg travel conditions are jointly adopted (clause 2.6.1); Skåne changed them alone, without prior information to partners | Moderate, if the file confirms no partner involvement |
| 1. Not lawfully made | Poorly prepared: no data, no legal review, case reused from an opposite assignment | Weak to moderate |
| 3. No power (fullmäktige) | Region-wide restriction contrary to trafikförsörjningsprogram goals is a matter of principle | Weak to moderate |
| 4. Breaches EU 2021/782 Art. 6 | Blanket ban exceeds a "restriction"; less restrictive options exist (DSB paid bike ticket) | Weak |
| 4. Breaches equal treatment (KL 2:3) | Folding bikes allowed, ordinary bikes not | Weak |

## Timing

The proposal says item 6 was to be **approved immediately**. The three-week appeal clock starts the day the approval is posted on the noticeboard, which may be within days. All grounds must be in the appeal before the deadline (KL 13:7); evidence can follow. File as early as possible so a suspension request can be decided before 1 November.
