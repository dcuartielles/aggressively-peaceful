# What happened to the 2022 "Cykel på tåg" assignment

Researched 2026-10-01 from documents published on skane.se. Read via web fetch; save the original PDFs in `inputs/political_decisions/`.

## Verdict

The report due in September 2022 was **not delivered on time** and was **never a real investigation**. What came back was a three-slide Skånetrafiken deck (2022-10-06) with no train data. It was filed under a **new case, 2022-POL000362**, which turned the question from *increasing* bike access to *reviewing the rules*, and first floated rush-hour, station and summer restrictions. **No report-back on the 2023 rules review has been found.** The 2026 ban was then filed under the **original 2022 case number**, 2022-POL000109, without referring to any of this.

## 3. Slide deck "Cykel på tåg", 2022-10-06

Source: [namndshandlingar 8191747](https://www.skane.se/namndshandlingar/8191747/). No author named. About 300 words; one number.

**Utmaningen (the challenge)**
- "Antalet cyklar ombord upplevs ha ökat, och det händer att kunder med cykel måste avvisas på grund av platsbrist"
- Bike places on trains are limited
- Shorter trains and cancelled departures in summer added to crowding
- Summer leisure travel with bikes loads certain stations
- "Det upplevs inte att det finns konkurrens mellan platser för personer med funktionsvariation och cyklar"

**Nationell utblick (national comparison)**
- "I Skåne finns det generellt sett mer plats för cyklar på tågen än i övriga Sverige. Ofta får det bara plats 2 cyklar per tågvagn i andra län."
- Some counties (Stockholm, UL) ban bikes at rush hour or at some stations

**Lösning – utan att ta betalt (solution without charging)**
- Strengthen information; trains running as several units are often not fully used; more station staff could help
- "Informationen till kunderna bör förtydligas": no guaranteed place, staff decide
- Possible further study: "Begränsning i tid (rusningstid)", "Begränsning i vilka stationer vi medger medtagande av cykel", "Eventuellt behov av 'sommarregler'"

All problems are described as "upplevs" (perceived). No passenger counts, incidents, costs or options for adding bike places.

## Why it matters

- **Contradiction on disabled passengers.** The 2022 deck says there is no perceived competition between bikes and places for people with disabilities. The 2026 proposal gives that competition as a reason for the ban.
- **Skåne had more bike space than the rest of Sweden** (2022 deck); the 2017 Cykelstrategi for Skåne wanted bike capacity maximised on the next generation of trains.
- **Four years, no data.** From 2022 to 2026 the only analysis on file is a 300-word deck of perceptions. The ban rests on that and the chair's proposal.
- **The case meant to increase access was used to ban.**
