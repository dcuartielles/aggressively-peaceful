Region Skåne · Kollektivtrafiknämnden · Ärende 2022-POL000109

A case opened to give bikes more room on trains ended in a ban.

From 1 November 2026, ordinary bikes may not board Pågatåg or Öresundståg in Skåne on weekday mornings and afternoons. We are documenting how that decision was made, what it was based on, and what the public record shows. Every claim here links to its source.

What we have found so far

  1. The ban was filed under a case meant to expand bike access. In April 2022 the committee asked for an investigation into how to increase access for bikes on Pågatåg. The 2026 ban carries the same case number. Minutes 7 April 2022, § 27 · Proposal 24 Sep 2026
  2. The promised investigation was a three-slide deck. It arrived late, in October 2022, described problems as "perceived", and contained one number. It also said bikes did not compete with places for disabled passengers, a reason the 2026 proposal gives for the ban. Skånetrafiken, "Cykel på tåg", 6 Oct 2022
  3. The 2026 decision rests on one document. The case file lists only the chair's proposal. It contains no passenger counts or incident figures, and states that legal review and union consultation were not considered necessary. Proposal 24 Sep 2026
  4. In 2024 the committee was told no further measures were needed. After the rules were tightened in 2023, the administration reported in February 2024 that the situation had improved significantly, that bike-related customer complaints were down 25 % on 2022, and that "no further measures are deemed necessary". Nothing in the 2026 case file explains what changed. Report-back 8 Feb 2024 · decision § 21, 8 Mar 2024
  5. Partner regions are told after the fact. Öresundståg is run jointly by six authorities. The proposal sends the decision to the other regions and Öresundståg AB once it is made; the cooperation agreement asks Skåne to inform the joint company before decisions that affect the whole system. On 2 October Region Kronoberg replied that it had received nothing, not even the decision, and that the ban was never up in the owners’ council, board or management group. Blekinge said discussions had taken place but no documents were produced. Samverkansavtal, clause 2.5.2
  6. The crowding was predicted when ticket prices were halved. In May an opposition member warned that a temporary price cut would bring overcrowding without new trains. In August Skånetrafiken acknowledged peak-hour crowding. In September the chair tied continued demand to the cheaper tickets. SVT 26 May · SVT 21 Aug · SVT 25 Sep

What constitutes a bike in Sweden

Swedish law defines a bike in the Road Traffic Definitions Act of 2001, lag (2001:559) om vägtrafikdefinitioner, 2 §. The definition is wider than most people expect. A "cykel" is any vehicle driven by pedals or a crank that is not a toy. It is also a pedal-assist e-bike whose motor only adds to the rider's own effort, stops helping above 25 km/h and has a continuous rated power of at most 250 watts. Two kinds of electric vehicle without pedals count too: one-person vehicles for people with a physical disability built for at most 20 km/h, and other one-person electric vehicles of the same kind that have at most 250 watts or balance themselves.

Transportstyrelsen's guidance gives examples for each point: e-bikes, electric wheelchairs and mobility scooters, electric kick scooters and Segways. An e-bike with a throttle or a stronger motor is no longer a bike but a moped. A child's kick scooter or tricycle usually counts as a toy. Nothing in the definition limits the number of wheels, the length or the number of riders, so cargo bikes and tandems are bikes as long as they meet these conditions.

This matters for the ban because the proposal the committee voted on never says what it means by "cykel". It only points to Skånetrafiken's travel conditions, under which children's bikes without a chain, folding bikes, folding kick scooters and micro bikes may still travel as hand luggage when folded. The statute is written for road traffic, and a transport authority may define the word differently in its travel conditions. The proposal does not do that, so it leaves open whether it covers, for example, a non-folding electric kick scooter or a cargo bike.

  1. 1. Pedal bikeDriven by pedals or a crank, and not a toy.
  2. 2. E-bikeMotor only assists pedalling, no help above 25 km/h, at most 250 W.
  3. 3. Mobility vehicleElectric, for people with a physical disability, one person, at most 20 km/h.
  4. 4. Light e-vehicleSame as 3, at most 250 W or self-balancing: e.g. an electric kick scooter.

How we work

Sources first

Every statement links to a public document, a law or a published article. When we could not verify something, we say so.

Names

We name elected politicians acting in office. Civil servants, train staff, commuters and other private people are described by their role.

What we do not publish

  • Email addresses and phone numbers
  • Names of people who answer our requests
  • Copies of original documents (we link to them)
  • Unverified tips and notes

The repository

The site is built from plain text files in a public Git repository, so every change is dated and visible.
