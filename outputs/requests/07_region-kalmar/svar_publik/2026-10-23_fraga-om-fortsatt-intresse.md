# Reply 2026-10-23 – Region Kalmar län (Kalmar länstrafik)

Answer to request 07, three weeks after it was sent. The registry forwarded the request to Kalmar länstrafik on 2026-10-02. On 2026-10-23 at 17:08, the deputy traffic director of Kalmar länstrafik wrote:

> Kollektivtrafiknämnden i Region Skåne har i dag meddelat att de river upp beslutet om cykelförbund ombord på Öresundståg. Är du trots detta fortsatt intresserad av att begära ut handlingarna?

(Our translation: "Region Skåne's public transport committee announced today that it is tearing up the decision on the bike ban on board Öresundståg. Are you still interested in requesting the documents?")

In short:
- Kalmar has not yet answered whether it holds any documents.
- Kalmar was told about the reversal on the day it was announced. We have no sign that Kalmar received anything before the original decision.
- A request for public documents does not lapse because the decision changes. The documents show how the partner regions were informed, and that question remains.
