# Reply 2026-10-26 – Region Kalmar län (Kalmar länstrafik): two documents, nothing else

Answer to request 07 (sent 2026-10-01), after we confirmed on 2026-10-26 that we still wanted the documents. The deputy traffic director of Kalmar länstrafik wrote at 16:01:

> Här kommer svar på din begäran.
>
> Bifogat hittar du:
>
> - Protokollsutdrag från kollektivtrafiknämnden i Region Skåne avseende förbud mot cyklar på tåg under högtrafik – inkom till Region Kalmar län 2026-10-08
> - Delegationsbeslut från kollektivtrafiknämnden i Region Skåne avseende upphävande av beslut om förbund mot cyklar på tåg under högtrafik – inkom till Region Kalmar län 2026-10-23
>
> Det finns inga andra handlingar inkomna till Region Kalmar län avseende Region Skånes beslut att förbjuda cyklar på tåg under högtrafik.

(Our translation: "Here is the answer to your request. Attached: the minutes extract from Region Skåne's public transport committee on the ban on bikes on trains at peak hours, received by Region Kalmar län on 8 October 2026; the delegated decision revoking it, received on 23 October 2026. There are no other documents received by Region Kalmar län about Region Skåne's decision to ban bikes on trains at peak hours.")

The two attachments are the certified extract of § 53, which we already have via Öresundståg AB, and the chair's decision of 23 October, which we already have via request 11. They are not republished here.

In short:
- **Kalmar received nothing before the decision.** No consultation, no information and no papers from Skåne. The only documents are the decision and its revocation.
- **The decision reached Kalmar a week after it was taken**, on 8 October, the day the minutes were signed and posted. This answers the open question of when the § 53 extract was sent: at least for Kalmar, on or just before 8 October. The certified extract itself carries no dispatch date.
- **The revocation reached Kalmar the day it was taken**, 23 October.
- Kalmar is one of the six authorities that own Öresundståg AB. The cooperation agreement (clause 2.5.2) asks Skåne to inform the joint company before decisions that affect the whole system.
