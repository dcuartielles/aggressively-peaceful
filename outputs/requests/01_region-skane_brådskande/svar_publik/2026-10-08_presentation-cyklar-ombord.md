# "Cyklar ombord" – Skånetrafiken's presentation to the committee, 2026-10-01

Released by Region Skåne's registry on 2026-10-08 in answer to request 01. 22 slides. This is the "PPT" the Green Party's reservation refers to ("Det har tillkommit statistik under dagens möte"). It is **not** listed among the documents in the case (the minutes list only "Beslutsförslag 2026-09-08").

Read from the preview on 2026-10-08. All 22 slides read.

## Slides 1–7: the problem

- **2 Problembeskrivning:** the problem is "mångfacetterat och hur man än gör blir någon drabbad"; crowding causes lower punctuality, irritation and conflicts; "Grupp ställs mot grupp, där de mest påstridiga tenderar att försöka få sin vilja igenom"; some routes worse than others; crowding varies over the day.
- **3 Vad händer med tryggheten?** Four headings: Pålitlighet, Ordning, Säkerhet, Miljö. Bullets: group set against group, sense of safety falls for those with and without bikes; staff affected; every extra second a train stands still affects a full line.
- **4 Vilka problem vill vi försöka lösa?** Give priority to those with the greatest need of a place when crowding is worst; reduce stress for staff (punctuality and conflicts); a restriction that is easy to understand and follow.
- **5 Hur stort är problemet?** "Pågatåg 2024: 249 ärenden, 2025: 277 ärenden, 2026: 392 ärenden. Öresundståg 2026: 310 ärenden. Kundtjänst 2026: 93 ärenden." The slide does not say what an "ärende" is (bike-related only? all incidents?), how they were counted, or whether 2026 is a full year. No figures for Öresundståg or customer service before 2026.
- **6 Hur ser beläggningen ut?** Chart "Högsta beläggning per timme" (% of seats). Peaks around 127 % at 07 and 107 % at 16; above 80 % roughly 05–07 and 13–17. No route, period or data source given.
- **7 Vilka sträckor är värst?** Pågatåg: 07:08 Åstorp–Hyllie 127 %; 06:41 Halmstad–Helsingborg C 105 %; 16:42 Hyllie–Åstorp 107 %; 15:49 Malmö C–Kävlinge 98 %; 17:12 Hyllie–Helsingborg C 93 %. "Observera att beläggningen beräknas i relation till antalet sittplatser ombord. Stående passagerare förekommer långt innan dess, ibland redan vid 50 % beläggning."

## Slides 8–15: occupancy per departure, weekdays 06–09 and 15–18

Bar charts of average occupancy (% of seats) per departure, with 80 % and 100 % lines, for: Lund–Malmö, Malmö–Lund, Helsingborg–Landskrona, Eslöv–Lund, Kristianstad–Hässleholm and others.

Observations (ours):
- The charts use the **proposal's** windows (06–09, 15–18), not the decided ones (06–08, 15:30–17:30).
- On several lines most departures in the window are **below 80 %**. Helsingborg–Landskrona: only a handful above 100 % (e.g. 07:59, 08:59, 16:10 at about 128 %), most between 15 % and 75 %. Kristianstad–Hässleholm: **no departure above about 67 %**. The ban still applies on every line and from every station in Skåne.
- The slides give no number of bikes per departure, so they do not show how much of the crowding bikes cause.

## Slides 16–21: options and recommendation

- **16 Handlingsalternativ** (section title).
- **17 Vad är ett bra handlingsalternativ?** Criteria: "Träffsäkert – minsta möjliga påverkan, högsta möjliga effekt"; easy to understand; easy to introduce; easy to follow up; "Vara bäst för flest".
- **18 Options:** 1 ban bikes on certain routes; 2 ban bikes at certain times; 3 ban bikes on one train system at certain times; 4 introduce a bike fee.
- **19 Värdering av respektive handlingsalternativ:** a table scoring each option on the five criteria with 0 / + / ++:

  | | Alt 1: Sträcka | Alt 2: Tider | Alt 3: Tågsystem | Alt 4: Avgift |
  | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
  | Träffsäkerhet | ++ | ++ | + | + |
  | Förstå | 0 | ++ | + | ++ |
  | Införa | ++ | ++ | ++ | 0 |
  | Följa upp | 0 | + | + | ++ |
  | Bäst för flest | + | + | 0 | + |
  | Summering | 5 | 8 | 5 | 6 |

- **20 Rekommendation:** "Förvaltningen föreslår kollektivtrafiknämnden att *i första hand* överväga att införa ett förbud mot cyklar på tåg under ett fast tidsintervall på vardagar."
- **21 Vad kan jag som kund göra?** Travel outside the ban hours; have a bike that counts as hand luggage; "Ha en cykel på varje station"; use bike hire or mobility services where they exist. Then: "**Det finns en risk för att en del kunder väljer bilen istället.** Några av alternativen ovan innebär extra kostnader för kunderna, vilket kan slå hårdare mot vissa än andra. De som väljer cykel istället för bil känner sig straffade/motarbetade."
- **22** Closing slide.

Observations (ours) on slides 16–21:
- **No safety criterion.** The options are scored on targeting, simplicity, introduction, follow-up and "best for most". Safety is not one of the criteria, although Skånetrafiken's newsletter of 2026-10-05 says the ban was decided "med hänsyn till säkerheten ombord".
- **Unweighted, unexplained scores.** The ratings are not explained, and "Träffsäkerhet ++" for a network-wide time ban sits awkwardly with the occupancy charts on slides 8–15, where several lines are well below capacity. A route ban (Alt 1) gets the same targeting score as a network-wide time ban.
- **Known negative effects, not assessed.** Slide 21 states that some customers may switch to the car and that extra costs fall harder on some groups. The proposal says "Beslutet bedöms inte få någon påverkan på miljön" and "inte få några ekonomiska konsekvenser". The administration's own presentation contradicts both.
- **Recommendation hedged.** "I första hand överväga" is weaker than the committee's outright ban.

## Questions this raises

1. What counts as an "ärende" in slide 5, and where do the 392 / 310 / 93 figures come from? Compare Seko klubb Pågatåg: 29 reports in 2026, 6 in the ban hours.
2. Why was this presentation, the only data, not in the case file sent to the committee before the meeting?
3. Why a network-wide ban when Skånetrafiken's own data shows several lines well below capacity in the ban hours?
4. How were the scores on slide 19 set, by whom, and why is safety not a criterion if safety is now the public reason?
5. Was the car-shift risk on slide 21 assessed anywhere? The proposal says there is no environmental impact.
