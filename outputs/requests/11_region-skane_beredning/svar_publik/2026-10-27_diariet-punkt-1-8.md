## Point by point

1. **What went out with the meeting notice:** the agenda and the 24 Sep proposal. The agenda lists one document for item 6: "Beslutsförslag 2026-09-24". **No data, no presentation.** The registry did not say when the notice was sent.
2. **What the preparatory committee saw (15 Sep):** a different draft, dated 8 Sep. The minutes (§ 17 c) only list "Förbud mot cyklar på tåg under högtrafik" among items prepared for the committee. They record no discussion, no data and no position. Six politicians took part, with the traffic director, the deputy traffic director, eight Skånetrafiken staff and two political secretaries.
3. **The minutes are wrong.** The Region confirms that the 1 October minutes give the wrong date for the proposal ("ska vara 260924"). Two versions existed: 8 Sep for the preparatory committee and 24 Sep for the committee.
4. **The presentation:** answered only with the case number. We were not told when members received it. Combined with point 1, it was not in the notice.
5. **Dispatch to the partners:** an email on **Thursday 8 October at 09:18**, seven days after the decision and about two hours after the minutes were signed. It went to the general mailboxes of the five regions and Öresundståg AB, with the one-line message "Översänder beslut från KTN 2026-10-01, § 53".
6. **Case log:** not provided. The answer "Diariet har svarat" ("the registry has answered") is unclear; we will ask again.
7. **Data behind the charts:** "Finns i beslutet 2026-10-01". **No underlying data was released.** There are no occupancy figures, no measurement period, and nothing for Öresundståg.
8. The chair's decision of 23 October (see our earlier reply file).

## What changed between the 8 Sep draft (seen by the preparatory committee) and the 24 Sep proposal (sent to the committee)

Text compared sentence by sentence, from the PDFs' text layers. The core is the same in both: the ban, the definition of peak hours (06.00–09.00 and 15.00–18.00), "samtliga tågsträckor och från samtliga stationer i Skåne", the exemptions in resevillkor 10.17, the boarding-only rule, "påverkas inte" for the Danish agreements, no environmental impact, no union consultation needed, and immediate approval.

**Added after the preparatory committee (only in the 24 Sep version):**
- Who is hit: "De kunder som framförallt påverkas negativt är de som är vana vid att kunna ta med cykeln under rusningstid, men även turister …"
- The comparisons: SL (Lidingöbanan, Roslagsbanan, Tvärbanan); Västtrafik, Alingsås–Göteborg; and Denmark ("krävs det bokning av cykelplats ombord på regionaltågen", and the Nørreport rule).
- The reasons for the chosen hours ("Valet av tidsintervaller beror på hur resandet ser ut …", morning versus afternoon peaks, Fridays).
- Follow-up: a report to the committee in the first quarter of 2027, with a proposal for summer 2027.

**Only in the 8 Sep version:**
- Follow-up "inom ramen för förvaltningens ordinarie verksamhet", meaning no report back to the committee.
- Minor wording and typos ("nögvändigt").

The 8 Sep draft is 3 pages; the 24 Sep proposal is 4. Both are signed by the chair and the traffic director, and both list only themselves as documents in the case.

## What this adds

- **Two drafts, both thin.** The preparatory committee saw an earlier version without the comparisons, the reasons for the hours and the follow-up. Those were added afterwards, by the chair and the traffic director, without returning to the preparatory committee. The claim about Denmark ("krävs det bokning"), which DSB's own rules contradict, was added at this stage.
- **The notice carried no data.** This is now confirmed by the Region's own documents, as required by the committee rules ("Med kallelsen ska följa beslutsunderlag").
- **The partners were told by a one-line email a week later.** This matches every partner reply: Kronoberg (2 Oct) and Halland (5 Oct) had nothing; Kalmar registered the decision on 8 Oct; Västtrafik had not had it passed on by 9 Oct.
- **No data behind the charts.** Asked for the data behind the occupancy charts, the Region points back to the decision itself.
