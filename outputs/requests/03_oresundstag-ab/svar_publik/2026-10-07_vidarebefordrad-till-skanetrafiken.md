In short: Öresundståg AB has not handled the request itself. It forwarded it to Skånetrafiken's registry and refers all questions there.

Note (ours): Öresundståg AB is a company owned by six regional transport authorities and is covered by the principle of public access through 2 kap. 3 § OSL. The request asks for **Öresundståg AB's own** documents (its management group, board and owners' council papers, and its incident reports). Skånetrafiken is one of the six owners and the authority whose decision is being examined. Whether Skånetrafiken can decide on access to Öresundståg AB's documents is unclear to us. We will ask Öresundståg AB to confirm who decides, and ask for a written decision if documents are withheld.
