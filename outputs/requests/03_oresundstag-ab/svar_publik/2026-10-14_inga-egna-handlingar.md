# Reply 2026-10-14 – Öresundståg AB

Answer to request 03 and our follow-up of 2026-10-08. Replied 12:02 by Öresundståg AB:

> Öresundståg AB har inga egna protokoll, beslut, beslutsunderlag, avvikelserapporter eller andra av bolaget upprättade handlingar som behandlar frågan om cykelförbudet.
>
> Bolaget har däremot mottagit Kollektivtrafiknämndens beslut från den 1 oktober 2026. Denna handling bifogas i sin helhet.
>
> Utöver den bifogade handlingen har Öresundståg AB inga handlingar som motsvarar din begäran avseende ärendet om cykelförbudet.

(Our translation: "Öresundståg AB has no minutes, decisions, decision papers, incident reports or other documents drawn up by the company that deal with the bike ban. The company has, however, received the committee's decision of 1 October 2026, attached in full. Apart from that, the company has no documents matching your request.")

The attachment is the certified extract of the minutes, § 53. Its distribution list reads: "Sänt till: Region Västra Götaland, Region Halland, Region Kronoberg, Region Kalmar, Region Blekinge samt Öresundståg". It carries no date of dispatch.

In short:
- The jointly owned company that runs Öresundståg produced **no** papers of its own on the ban: nothing in its management group, board or owners' council, and no incident reports.
- Its only document is the decision, which it received **after** it was taken.
- The cooperation agreement (clause 2.5.2) asks Skåne to inform the company's management group before decisions that affect the whole system. The company's own records show no such step.
- The extract lists all five partner regions as recipients. Kronoberg (2 Oct), Halland (5 Oct) and Västtrafik for Västra Götaland (9 Oct) all said they had not received it.
