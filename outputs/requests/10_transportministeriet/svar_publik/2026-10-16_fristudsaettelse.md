# Reply 2026-10-16 – Transportministeriet (Denmark), deadline extended

The Danish ministry's legal office ([kontorfunktionær]) wrote on 2026-10-16 about our access request, case 2026-4873:

> By-, Land- og Transportministeriet har desværre ikke været i stand til at besvare din anmodning om aktindsigt med sagsnr.: 2026-4873 inden for 7 arbejdsdage, jf. offentlighedslovens § 36, stk. 2. Det skyldes, at ministeriet er i gang med at behandle andre aktindsigtssager, der er modtaget før din anmodning.
>
> Ministeriet forventer at besvare din anmodning senest den 13. november 2026.

(Our translation: "The ministry has unfortunately not been able to answer your access request within 7 working days, cf. section 36(2) of the Danish Access to Public Administration Files Act, because it is handling other access requests received before yours. The ministry expects to answer by 13 November 2026 at the latest.")

In short: no documents yet, and no refusal. The answer is expected after the ban takes effect (1 November) and after the Swedish appeal deadline (29 October). The ministry gave the reason for the delay, as the Danish act requires.
